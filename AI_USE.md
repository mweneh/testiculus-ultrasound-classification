# AI Use Disclosure

## AI Tools Used

I used OpenAI Codex as an implementation and testing assistant during
development of this capstone. I also used Claude Code as a separate review and
audit aid, particularly for deployment options, release preparation and
submission-readiness checks. These tools contributed to the repository's
software and documentation; they were not treated as authors of the research
argument or as substitutes for my academic judgement.

## Nature of AI Assistance


The assistance covered checkpoint-based inference, Grad-CAM integration, and Docker Compose configuration.

For the scientific work, AI assistance  supported
the documented post-hoc exploratory MobileNetV3-Small extension after the
original Custom CNN and ResNet18 test outcomes were known. 


## Verification and Academic Responsibility

AI-generated suggestions and code were subject to review through targeted
tests, saved experiment records, artifact and metric checks, report checks,
container checks and independent audit of the final evidence.
